FINAL CHECKLIST
Check your finished campaign
Review your photos and video against each point before saving the final set.
The product is recognisable
Its shape, colour and important details match the selected product reference in your photos and
video.
The three photos offer useful views
The set shows the product clearly from different views, with a consistent look and your chosen
location.
The creator and setting stay consistent
When using a creator, the person and outfit match the chosen sheet. The setting matches the
selected location.
The action, camera and ending are clear
The product action is easy to follow and the camera keeps it visible. The final action or spoken line
finishes before the cut.
Sound supports the action
Speech is clear and matches the visible action. In a commercial, the sound supports the movement.
The creative choice fits the audience
The look and format suit your intended viewer and make the product's appeal clear. The product
remains the focus.
SAVE YOUR CAMPAIGN
A working draft is enough. If a moment distracts from the product, keep this version and choose
one specific fix. Save the original product photo, three selected photos and your chosen video
together.
