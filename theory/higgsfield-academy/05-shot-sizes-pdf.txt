@location_1 defines the setting. Keep each person and the object consistent with their references.
Use the same mentions throughout the prompt. Name the person before each action; state who owns or

03
SHOTS AND TIMING
Build a scene from visible
beats
Decide what must happen, then choose the views that make each change clear.
01
DEFINE THE ACTION
Write the starting state, the visible changes and the ending. Name who acts and what changes;
keep the actions in the order they must happen.
02
CHOOSE THE VIEW
Choose a shot size and angle for each beat. Keep hands and objects visible for an interaction;
move closer when a reaction becomes the important information.
03
PLAN CUTS AND MOVEMENT
Give each cut a new shot number. Within a shot, choose a static camera or describe its move
and destination. Several actions can share one continuous view.
04
ASSIGN THE TIME
Give each shot a start and end time. Allow time for actions, dialogue and any final hold. The final
end time should match the duration you select.
Keep these three choices separate
SIZE
How much of the subject is visible.
ANGLE
The height and direction from which we see it.
MOVEMENT
How the camera changes position or viewing direction.
CHECK THE PACE
Watch the result at normal speed. If a beat is rushed, simplify the action or allow more time. If an
action is hidden, revise the view that needs to show it.

Use the result to revise the
prompt
Save the tested prompt and take. Write down what happened and what you wanted instead.
TAKE 1 / UPRIGHT
TAKE 2 / FLAT
Turn each mismatch into a clear instruction. For example, if a case stands upright when it should lie flat,
specify its orientation and supporting surface. You can address several errors in the same revision.
Ask an LLM to help with the wording
Give Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or AI Director the exact prompt you tested, your error list and the intended
result. Explain the problems in everyday language if you do not know the camera or prompting terms.
PROMPT REPAIR REQUEST
Revise the tested prompt below. What went wrong: [describe the errors]. What I want instead:
[describe the intended result]. Keep the parts that already work. Update every affected line so the
instructions agree. Return the full revised prompt, then list exactly what you changed or added and
why.
Tested prompt: [paste the original prompt].
Read the revised prompt and the change list. Check that the assistant kept your intent and did not add
unwanted actions or camera moves. Generate again, then review the whole new take with sound.

YOUR SCENE TEMPLATE
Your scene template
Use your selected @ mentions. Fill in the scene, actions and timing in plain language.
COPY & ADAPT
SCENE CONTEXT
@character_1 and @character_2 are in @location_1. Starting state: [positions and situation]. Action
and ending: [what changes, in order, and the visible outcome].
REFERENCES
@character_1 and @character_2: identity and clothing. @prop_1: object appearance; carried by
@character_1. @location_1: space, light and colour.
SHOTS
Shot 1, [start-end time]: [shot size and angle]. Camera: [static, or movement with a destination].
@character_1 [visible action]. Shot 2, [start-end time]: [next view and camera behaviour].
@character_2 [action or reaction]. @character_2 says: "[dialogue]".
CONTINUITY & SOUND
@prop_1: [position and orientation, if needed]. [Other details that must stay consistent]. [Ambience,
action sounds and music instruction]. [Final hold, if needed].
Add a shot when you need a cut; keep one shot block for a continuous view. Remove unused references
and sections. Check that Scene context and the shot instructions describe the same sequence.
Before generating, match the selected duration to the final timecode and check the attached assets,
framing, audio and displayed cost.

WHAT SHOULD YOU FIX?
Choose the next step
Check the input before paying for another attempt.
Does the same error return across takes?
YES: inspect the prompt and source assets first. NO: compare the takes; an isolated defect may justify
another attempt.
01 / FIX THE ASSET
The unwanted appearance is already in the reference. Correct that image before reusing it; do not rely
on extra wording to erase a visible detail.
02 / FIX THE PROMPT
The wrong person acts, a prop is misplaced, or camera instructions conflict. Name the actor, state the
order, and make the related lines agree.
03 / GENERATE ANOTHER TAKE
The inputs are clear but this take has an isolated defect, or you want a different staging or sequence.
Keep the original and compare the whole new clip.
Check three things
Action: did the named person do the intended action? Continuity: do the people, prop and place remain
recognisable? Camera: does the framing keep the important action visible?
LESSON EXERCISE
Save Take 1 and its prompt. List the errors and the result you want. Revise the relevant instructions,
generate Take 2, and compare both clips with sound. Submit both takes and prompts under Lesson
5.
